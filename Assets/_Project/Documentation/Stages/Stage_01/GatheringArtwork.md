# Stage One gathering artwork

The eight source textures were made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The two playable plants use transparent cutouts. Their 00–10 frame sets were baked from those cutouts on a shared square canvas, with a fixed soil line, increasing lift, and a small alternating tilt. Frame 00 hides the roots; frame 10 lifts the entire root above the soil. Both sets share the same woodland background.

## Generation prompts

### CodexPanel

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset: a wide 16:9 open book background texture for a Korean historical fantasy game's botanical codex. Orthographic straight overhead flat spread, symmetrical landscape, two large blank pale warm ivory hanji paper pages, faint center crease, very fine botanical ornamental corner flourishes only in outermost margins, thin worn dark pine-green cloth cover barely visible at edges with small gold corners. Pages must fill nearly the whole frame and stay almost blank, light and even, for UI text. Tight crop to book cover with all four corners visible, no external props. No words, letters, illustrations in writing areas, book tabs or glyphs.

### GatherBackground

Use case: stylized-concept. Landscape 16:9 close-up game UI environment painting of a Korean woodland forest floor, mossy rocks framing left and right edges, dappled warm light, dark subdued forest greens in far background, soft deep green canopy bokeh. A small flat bare patch of crumbly dark umber earth at lower center, roughly three quarters of the way down the picture, where a separate foreground plant sprite will be composited. Keep exact center open and simple so herb stands out, lower strip quiet and dark. Painterly realistic historical Korean fantasy game, golden-hour forest lighting, no central plant, no character, no hands, no text.

### LeafHerb

Botanical game sprite, softly painterly realistic historical Korean woodland, leaf herb with long thin pointed lanceolate emerald and lime leaves on a few slender branching stems, no flowers, tips and veins finely drawn. A clear stem-to-root junction and a short but clearly visible beige tapered root bundle with several hair roots. Single whole plant isolated upright on transparent background, centered, ample clear padding on every side, complete roots and leaves. No scene, no soil, no objects, no hand, no lettering. Clear silhouette, watercolor and fine ink.
