## Placing cloth and jump pads

Drag a **variant**, not a `_Base` template, from `Assets/_Project/Prefabs/InteractiveObjects/Stages/Stage_01` into the stage scene:

- `PF_HangingCloth_Black`, `Blue_A`, `Blue_B`, `Red`, `White_A`, `White_B`, and `Yellow` each contain their rendered fabric and `GripPoint`. Move or scale the prefab root to adjust both together; the visual and grip point swing around that root as one unit. The authored `GripPoint` position at play start sets the resting grab point and swing length; move its **Position** to change where players grab. Its **Scale** does not move the point. Select the cloth to see the yellow grab-point guide in Scene view, and adjust `_grabRange` separately if players cannot reach it. `_length` is only a fallback when the authored `GripPoint` sits at the root pivot.
- Cloth W/S input pushes along the camera's horizontal forward/backward axis. A reverse press shortly before the swing turns is buffered until the return starts. Select the placed cloth or its prefab variant and tune `HangingCloth` in the Inspector: `_maxAngle` is the main swing angle limit (degrees), `_bendMaxAngle` is the extra angle after the cloth touches a branch, and `_maxSwingSpeed` caps angular speed in degrees/second for both swings (`0` = no cap, preserving the existing feel). `_pumpImpulseSeconds` and `_driveAcceleration` set the force added per press, `_spring` pulls the cloth toward its resting angle, `_damping` slows it over time, and `_reversalInputWindow` allows an early reverse press near the turning point. `_releaseSpeed` scales the velocity transferred to a player who lets go. Shared defaults are on `PF_HangingCloth_Base`; tune a single variant or scene instance to override just that cloth.
- `PF_MushroomJumpPad` and `PF_BranchJumpPad` each contain the model, leaf socket, water inlet, and water jet. Their root pivot is at the leaf launch point. Move or rotate the root to place the whole assembly; adjust `WallWaterInlet`/`BranchWaterInlet`, `SapJet`/`BranchSapJet`, and the model's `LeafCenter` only when changing their internal layout. The selected prefab shows the inlet in cyan and the leaf/water outlet in green.

New jump-pad instances receive a unique leaf-target ID in the Editor after placement or duplication. `Tools > Herbalist > Stage 1 > Assign Jump Pad IDs` runs the same check manually. Save the scene after placing them so multiplayer peers use the same authored ID. The original fabric FBX is still the mesh source; its separate scene instance is no longer needed.
